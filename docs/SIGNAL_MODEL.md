# NiftyEdge Pro — Signal Model

## Overview

Each signal is generated by evaluating 9 independent market factors, scoring each one, applying adaptive weights, and summing to a final score between −14 and +14.

```
Signal Score = Σ (factor_raw_score × adaptive_weight) + bonus_factors
```

- Score ≥ **+3** → **BUY**
- Score ≤ **−3** → **SELL**
- Otherwise → **NEUTRAL**

---

## The 9 Factors

### 1. PCR — Put-Call Ratio
**What it measures:** Sentiment of option writers (smart money).

| PCR Value | Raw Score | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| > 1.4 | +2 | Heavy put writing — strong bullish |
| 1.2 – 1.4 | +1 | Moderate put writing — mild bullish |
| 1.0 – 1.2 | +0.5 | Slight put bias |
| 0.8 – 1.0 | −0.5 | Slight call bias |
| 0.6 – 0.8 | −1 | Moderate call writing — mild bearish |
| < 0.6 | −2 | Heavy call writing — strong bearish |

**Why it works:** When PCR is high, option writers are selling puts — they expect the market to hold. Writers typically have more capital and information than buyers.

---

### 2. Bull Probability
**What it measures:** Statistical probability of a bullish close, derived from Black-Scholes implied volatility.

```
P(bullish) = N( (spot - ATM + 0.5σ) / σ )
where σ = spot × IV × √(T/365)
```

| Probability | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| > 65% | +2 |
| 58 – 65% | +1 |
| 42 – 58% | 0 |
| 35 – 42% | −1 |
| < 35% | −2 |

---

### 3. OI Change Pattern
**What it measures:** Whether smart money is entering or exiting positions.

| Pattern | Raw Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Both Call OI and Put OI declining | +2 | Short covering → sharp move up likely |
| Call OI rising, Put OI falling | +1 | Fresh long call buying |
| Put OI rising (>5%), Call OI falling | −2 | Fresh put buying → bearish |
| Call OI falling, Put OI rising | −1 | Call unwinding + put building |

---

### 4. Max Pain Distance
**What it measures:** How far the current spot is from Max Pain (the price at which option writers lose the least).

```
Max Pain = strike with minimum total option writer losses
```

Option writers actively drive the market toward max pain near expiry.

| Distance from Max Pain | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| Spot < Max Pain by > 2 strikes | +1.5 |
| Spot < Max Pain by 1 strike | +1 |
| Near Max Pain | 0 |
| Spot > Max Pain by 1 strike | −1 |
| Spot > Max Pain by > 2 strikes | −1.5 |

---

### 5. EMA Trend
**What it measures:** Short-term price momentum structure using Exponential Moving Averages.

- **EMA9** = 9-period EMA of candle close prices
- **EMA21** = 21-period EMA of candle close prices

| Condition | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| EMA9 > EMA21 AND last candle bullish | +2 |
| EMA9 > EMA21 only | +1 |
| EMA9 < EMA21 AND last candle bearish | −2 |
| EMA9 < EMA21 only | −1 |
| EMAs near equal | 0 |

---

### 6. RSI (14-period)
**What it measures:** Whether the market is overbought or oversold.

| RSI | Raw Score | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| < 30 | +1.5 | Oversold — high probability of bounce |
| 30 – 40 | +0.5 | Mildly oversold |
| 40 – 60 | 0 | Neutral |
| 60 – 70 | −0.5 | Mildly overbought |
| > 70 | −1.5 | Overbought — high probability of pullback |

---

### 7. Momentum (Rate of Change)
**What it measures:** Speed and direction of price movement over the selected timeframe.

```
Momentum = (Close[last] - Close[first]) / Close[first] × 100
```

| Momentum | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| > +0.5% | +1 |
| +0.2% to +0.5% | +0.5 |
| −0.2% to +0.2% | 0 |
| −0.5% to −0.2% | −0.5 |
| < −0.5% | −1 |

---

### 8. VWAP Position
**What it measures:** Whether the current price is above or below the institutional average price.

```
VWAP = Σ((High + Low + Close) / 3) / n
```

| Condition | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| Spot > VWAP by > 0.3% | +0.5 |
| Spot near VWAP | 0 |
| Spot < VWAP by > 0.3% | −0.5 |

---

### 9. IV Rank
**What it measures:** Whether implied volatility is high or low relative to recent history.

| ATM IV | Raw Score | Strategy implication |
|---|---|---|
| < 12% | +0.5 | Low IV → options are cheap → buy bias |
| 12% – 25% | 0 | Normal IV |
| > 25% | −0.5 | High IV → options expensive → sell bias |

---

## Bonus Factors

### Bollinger Band Breakout
- Spot breaks above upper BB (+2σ) → **+1** (momentum breakout)
- Spot breaks below lower BB (−2σ) → **−1** (breakdown)

### Stochastic K Confirmation
- Stoch K < 20 AND trend is bullish → **+0.5** (confluence)
- Stoch K > 80 AND trend is bearish → **−0.5** (confluence)

## Timeframe-Specific R:R Ratios

| Timeframe | Target Multiplier | SL Multiplier | Min R:R |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 Min | 1.25× | 0.75× | 1:1 |
| 20 Min | 1.40× | 0.70× | 1:1.33 |
| 30 Min | 1.50× | 0.67× | 1:1.5 |
| 1 Hour | 1.65× | 0.62× | 1:1.72 |
| 3 Hour | 1.85× | 0.58× | 1:2.07 |

Longer timeframes offer better R:R because there's more time for the trade to develop.

## Confidence Score

The confidence percentage shown on each tip reflects:

```
confidence = (factor_alignment% × 0.6) + (|score|/14 × 0.33 × 100) + 40
```

Capped at 93% (models are never 100% certain).

---

## Limitations

- Signals are based on **statistical patterns**, not guarantees
- The model performs best during active market hours (09:15–15:30 IST)
- Low candle counts (<5) reduce indicator reliability
- Sudden news events cannot be predicted by any technical model
